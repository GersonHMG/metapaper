# Experiment B2: TCN on a frozen spatial module

**0 layers** = the pretrained model (B's 0-layer model). **1–3 layers** = its spatial module frozen (eval mode, no gradients), new TCN + head trained on top. See `experiments/experiment_b2_frozen_spatial.md`.

`AsymSETNetGroupedSegments`: grouped spatial module per segment, non-causal TCN (width 32), Linear-ReLU-Linear head per segment; window probability = mean of the segment probabilities. Recording CV (5 folds, leave-one-recording-out when a patient has fewer recordings), 24 patients, chb16 excluded at 10 s. See `experiments/experiment_b.md`.

Per-fold rows with all metrics: `b2_frozen_spatial_folds.csv`. Cells are F1 mean ± variance across patients unless stated otherwise.
